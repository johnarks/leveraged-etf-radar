Method: 12 oscillators (RSI-14, Stoch %K, CCI-20, ADX-14, Awesome Oscillator, Momentum-10, MACD, Stoch RSI, Williams %R, Ultimate Oscillator, Bull Power, Bear Power) + 12 moving averages (SMA/EMA 10/20/30/50/100/200). Score = 50 + 50×(buys−sells)/24, mapped to Strong Buy ≥80, Buy ≥60, Neutral ≥40, Sell ≥20, Strong Sell <20. Data: Yahoo Finance daily bars.
Sentiment (1–10) = 50% theme/group news (recent headlines for the sector, industry, country, or asset group the ETF tracks) + 50% top-10-holdings news (individual company news for the proxy's top 10 holdings, weighted by position size; holdings without individual coverage inherit the theme score). Single-stock ETFs use the stock's own company news; bullion/bond/futures proxies use the theme score for both halves. Coverage % = share of top-10 weight with individual company news. Labels: 9–10 very bullish, 7–8.9 bullish, 5.5–6.9 slightly bullish, 4.5–5.4 neutral, 3–4.4 slightly bearish, 1–2.9 bearish.
Limitations: only the top 10 holdings are analyzed, and most proxy holdings files contain just the top 25 positions — not full constituents. Scores reflect news as of — and are a snapshot, not a forecast.
